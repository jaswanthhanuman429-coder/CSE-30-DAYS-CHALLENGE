#LOOPLAB - Daily Expense Analyzer
**LOOPLAB** about to analyses daily expenses and read expense amount until the user enters 0. 
Use of loops, conditional statements, arithmetic operators, and variables(Without arrays, functions, and strings).

EXPENSE AMOUNT            CATEGORY            ACTION                           
below ₹100                small              count and add to Small Total
₹100 to below ₹500        medium             count and add to Medium Total
₹500 or above             large              count and add to Large Total
exactly ₹0                Stop signal        Stop;do not count orTotal
Negative amount           Invalid            Display an error; do not count or add

##Required Summary
display
(a)number of expenses
(b)total of expenses
(c)count and total for Small, Medium, and Large expenses
(d)average expense
